Fes

Step Back in Time in Fes: The Spiritual Capital

Fes is the oldest of Morocco's four Imperial Cities and is widely considered the country's spiritual and cultural heart. Home to the world's oldest university and the most intact medieval city in the Arab world, Fes is a destination for those who want to experience the authentic history of Morocco.

The Legendary Fes el-Bali Medina

A UNESCO World Heritage site, Fes el-Bali is the world's largest car-free urban area. Navigating its 9,000 narrow alleyways is like traveling back a thousand years. You'll hear the calls of artisans, the smell of exotic spices, and see donkeys carrying goods through the labyrinthine streets.

Top Historic Landmarks in Fes

  • Al-Qarawiyyin University: Founded in 859 AD, it is recognized by UNESCO as the oldest continuously operating educational institution in the world.
  • The Chouara Tannery: Witness the ancient process of leather dyeing in massive stone vessels, a scene that hasn't changed for centuries.
  • Bou Inania Madrasa: An architectural masterpiece featuring stunning examples of Merenid woodcarving and zellij tilework.
  • The Royal Palace (Dar el-Makhzen): While you can't go inside, its seven magnificent golden doors are a masterpiece of Moroccan craftsmanship.

Artisans and Traditional Crafts

Fes is famous for its highly skilled artisans. Our guided Fes walking tours include visits to specialized souks for pottery, weaving, and copper work. You'll have the chance to see masters at work and find high-quality, authentic souvenirs to take home.

The Gastronomy of Fes

Fessi cuisine is considered some of the finest in Morocco. From the savory Pastilla (meat pie) to the unique Smen (aged butter), a food tour in Fes is an essential experience for any culinary traveler.
