Tangier

The Gateway Connecting Two Continents

Situated at the Strait of Gibraltar where the Mediterranean Sea meets the Atlantic Ocean, Tangier has long served as the historic gateway connecting Europe and Africa. Its strategic location has given it a unique, multicultural history.

Explore the Kasbah and the bustling Medina, which have inspired generations of artists, writers, and musicians. The city boasts a fascinating blend of Moroccan, Spanish, and French influences visible in its architecture and cuisine.

Just outside the city, you can visit the legendary Caves of Hercules and Cape Spartel, where the two great bodies of water meet, offering some of the most spectacular coastal views in North Africa.
