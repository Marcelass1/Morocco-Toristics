Essaouira

Relax in Essaouira: The Windy City of the Atlantic

Essaouira is Morocco's most charming coastal destination, known for its relaxed atmosphere, artistic community, and historic ramparts. Originally named "Mogador" by Portuguese explorers, this UNESCO World Heritage city is a favorite for those looking to escape the heat of the desert and enjoy the cool Atlantic breeze.

The Historic Port and Ramparts

The city's iconic Skala de la Ville ramparts offer breathtaking views of the ocean and have even served as a filming location for *Game of Thrones*. Below the walls lies the bustling fishing port, where you can watch traditional blue wooden boats bring in the daily catch. For an authentic experience, pick your fresh fish at the market and have it grilled for you on the spot!

Kitesurfing and Water Sports

Thanks to the "Alizé" (trade winds), Essaouira is one of the world's premier destinations for kitesurfing, windsurfing, and surfing. Whether you're a seasoned pro or a complete beginner, the wide sandy beaches offer the perfect conditions for water sports. We can arrange private lessons and equipment rentals for your stay.

A Hub for Arts and Music

Essaouira has long been a sanctuary for artists and musicians. The city is the center of the soulful Gnawa music tradition and hosts the world-famous Gnawa and World Music Festival every summer. Wander through the Medina to find unique thuya wood carvings, contemporary art galleries, and handcrafted jewelry.

Best Day Trips from Marrakech

Located just 3 hours from Marrakech, Essaouira is one of the most popular day trip destinations. Our Essaouira tours include a stop at a local Argan Oil cooperative, where you can see the famous "climbing goats" and learn how the precious oil is produced.
