Casablanca

Casablanca: Where Modernity Meets Tradition

Casablanca is the economic and industrial engine of Morocco, offering a dynamic and cosmopolitan atmosphere that differs greatly from the traditional imperial cities. It is a city of grand boulevards, Art Deco architecture, and spectacular coastal views along the Atlantic Ocean.

The Magnificent Hassan II Mosque

No visit to Casablanca is complete without seeing the Hassan II Mosque. It is one of the largest mosques in the world and a true masterpiece of Moroccan craftsmanship.

  • Stunning Location: Built on a promontory overlooking the Atlantic Ocean, part of its floor is made of glass, allowing worshippers to kneel over the sea.
  • The Tallest Minaret: At 210 meters, it is an iconic landmark visible from across the city.
  • Open to All: It is one of the few mosques in Morocco that allows non-Muslim visitors inside via guided tours.

Explore the Corniche and Coastal Life

The Ain Diab Corniche is the city's entertainment hub, perfect for a sunset stroll, world-class dining, or enjoying the Atlantic breeze. The area is lined with high-end beach clubs and modern malls, showcasing the forward-looking face of modern Morocco.

Art Deco Architecture and the Habous Quarter

For history buffs, the city center features a unique blend of French colonial Art Deco architecture and traditional Moroccan design. To experience a more traditional side of the city, visit the Habous Quarter (New Medina), designed in the 1920s to look like a traditional Moroccan city, filled with bookshops and pastry stalls.
